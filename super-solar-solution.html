    From site survey to subsidy

    1. Site surveyWe check your roof, shade and electricity load.
    2. ApplyRegistration and subsidy application on the national rooftop solar portal.
    3. InstallationPanels, inverter and structure go up on your roof.
    4. Net meterYour electricity company inspects, fits the net meter and commissions the plant.
    5. Subsidy creditedThe central subsidy reaches your bank account after verification.

    Super Solar Solution is an independent solar installer. This is not a government website.

    Figures are estimates for a typical home. The final price depends on your roof, equipment and a site survey. Central subsidy follows the PM Surya Ghar residential structure: ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW, ₹18,000 for the third kW, capped at ₹78,000. State top-ups, loan terms and scheme dates change, so confirm current details before you buy.

    Assumptions: 120 units per kW per month, panel output falls 0.7% a year, electricity rates rise 3% a year, only units you would otherwise buy from the grid are counted, about 100 sq ft of roof per kW, 0.82 kg CO₂ per unit of grid power, 21 kg CO₂ absorbed by a tree in a year. Fixed charges on your bill are not included.
